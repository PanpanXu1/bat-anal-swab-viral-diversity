# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a respectful and constructive environment for
discussion, reproducibility review and technical contribution.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Focus criticism on methods, code, documentation and evidence.
- Assume good faith while asking for clarification.
- Avoid posting sensitive locality information, private data or personal
  information in public discussions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruptive behavior.
- Public disclosure of sensitive cave roost locations, private site-use
  information or controlled-access data.

## Enforcement

Concerns can be reported to Dr. Zhiqiang Wu at zqwu_lab@163.com. Reports will
be reviewed with attention to participant safety, data sensitivity and the
integrity of the research record.
